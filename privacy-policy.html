Privacy Policy

For the TimeZone Converter browser extension.

Summary

TimeZone Converter processes page text locally in your browser to detect and convert time expressions. The extension does not sell personal data, run analytics, or include trackers.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data.

Data We Store

The extension stores your settings (such as highlight preferences, ignored sites, scan mode, and formatting options) using chrome.storage.sync. This allows settings to sync between browsers where you are signed into Chrome.

Page Content

The extension reads visible text content on pages you visit in order to find time expressions (e.g. 8PM CET) and then highlight/convert them. This processing happens locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The core functionality does not require any external network requests. If you click the support button, you may be taken to a third-party website (e.g. Buy Me a Coffee).

Changes

This policy may be updated as the extension evolves. The latest version should be published along with extension updates.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, contact the developer via the extension listing/support page.
