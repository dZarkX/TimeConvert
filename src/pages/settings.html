Privacy

This extension processes detected times directly in your browser. It does not sell or share personal data.

  • Settings are stored in chrome.storage.sync (so they can sync between your browsers).
  • No analytics, trackers, or ads.
  • No external API calls are required for basic functionality.
  • The extension only reads page text to find time expressions and highlight/convert them.

About

TimeZone Converter v1.3.2

Automatically detects and converts time zones on web pages to your local time.

Features:

  • The extension only reads page text to find time expressions and highlight/convert them.
  • Use 24-hour format
  • Click to toggle (original ↔ converted)
  • Customize how detected times appear on pages
