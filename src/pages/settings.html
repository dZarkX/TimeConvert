About

TimeZone Converter v1.0.0

Automatically detects and converts time zones on web pages to your local time.

Features:

  • Auto-detection of times with timezone abbreviations
  • Support for 12-hour and 24-hour formats
  • 50+ timezone abbreviations supported
  • Click to toggle between original and converted time
  • Customizable highlight colors
